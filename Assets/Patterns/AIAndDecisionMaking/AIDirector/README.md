# AI Director

**Category:** AI & Decision Making · **Site slug:** `director-ai`

> A meta-AI that paces encounters by watching player stress and adjusting spawns.

## What this sample will show

WithoutPattern builds a small feature the straightforward way and runs into the problem the page describes. WithPattern builds the same feature with AI Director, so you can run both and compare them.

The problem, from the page:

> Encounters are scripted: the same 12 enemies spawn at the same doorway every time. Skilled players are bored, struggling players are overwhelmed, and on the second playthrough everyone knows what is coming.
