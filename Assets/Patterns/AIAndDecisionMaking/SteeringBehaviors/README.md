# Steering Behaviors

**Category:** AI & Decision Making · **Site slug:** `steering-behaviors`

> Combine simple forces (seek, flee, arrive, avoid) into lifelike movement.

## What this sample will show

WithoutPattern builds a small feature the straightforward way and runs into the problem the page describes. WithPattern builds the same feature with Steering Behaviors, so you can run both and compare them.

The problem, from the page:

> An enemy that moves its position straight toward the player at a fixed speed moves like a cursor. It turns instantly, overshoots or stops dead, walks through other enemies, and twenty of them stack into one blob.
